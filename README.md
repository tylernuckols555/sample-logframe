# Human-elephant coexistence results chain (Conservation Standards v5)

An interactive results chain (theory of change) for a human-elephant coexistence programme in Kanchanaburi Province, Thailand: the southern Western Forest Complex (sWEFCOM), delivered by ZSL Thailand under Darwin Initiative 30-003. It sets out how diversified, elephant-friendly agroforestry is intended to reduce crop raiding and retaliatory killing of elephants while securing farmer livelihoods, and it maps the social safeguards (ZSL FAIRER / GEDSI) that determine whether those outcomes are equitable, not only aggregate. The diagram uses CMP Conservation Standards v5 vocabulary: actions, outputs, intermediate results, threat-reduction results, and biodiversity and human-wellbeing focal values, with assumptions on the links and goals and objectives attached to key results. The programme's Darwin logframe outputs sit inside the same chain, so donor and Conservation Standards logic read as one diagram. A companion situation model shows the governance and climate drivers behind the current state.

Hover any box to light up its direct links; select a box to trace the full causal chain and open its detail: activities, indicators, the assumption on its link, and the attached safeguard. A guided tour walks the theory of change one link at a time.
